## Interpretation and research decision

The after-publication information source is directly compatible with the task;
there is no reason to keep the earlier mandatory-before-publication restriction.
Exact target indexing is not fully disambiguated by the page/Q&A. The least
assumption-heavy interpretation starts the prediction target from the LAST
PUBLISHED value; therefore publication-reference AP1 is the conservative
case-facing track. Effective-reference AP1 is retained as a separately labelled
conditional scenario, never substituted silently because its metric is higher.
Q&A Sep5p5 allows team indexing assumptions and states information availability;
it is not explicit approval of evaluating from the older effective price.

This is a team interpretation of the primary sources, not an organizer's approval
of our implementation. A real bank-price dataset is NOT a prerequisite for the
hackathon's public-rate scorecard; do not stall model experiments on that gap.
Do not claim actual execution savings or a guaranteed stale-price window.

Current PDF applicability report predates this direct-page recheck. Its strong
need for bank quotes applies to proving real-world monetary benefit, not to
permission to optimize the public-rate target in the case. Carry this distinction
into subsequent reports.
